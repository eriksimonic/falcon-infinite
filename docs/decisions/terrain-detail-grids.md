# Architecture Decision: Real-World 100×100 km Terrain & Detail Grids

## Status
Accepted — validated on the **Vipava Valley** (`100 × 100 km`, centered on `LJAJ` Ajdovščina `45.8878°N, 13.8869°E`).

## Context
To make real-world theaters feel authentic in a zero-backend, GitHub-Pages-hosted Three.js flight simulator while maintaining locked 60 FPS and instant page loads, we need a repeatable baking & runtime streaming pipeline for every future `100 × 100 km` real-world grid.

## Decision: 4-Layer Baked Regional Grid Pipeline

Every real-world `100 × 100 km` theater (`[-50 km, +50 km]` around a primary home airfield at `(0, 0)`) is baked once offline into four compact static assets under `public/media/<region>_*` and streamed cleanly at runtime:

| Layer | Asset | Source (Free, Zero API Key) | Resolution / Budget | Runtime Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Elevation (DEM)** | `<region>_dem.png` + `<region>_meta.json` | AWS Open Terrain Tiles (Terrarium RGB PNGs, Zoom 10–11) | `1024×1024` (~98 m/sample, `~1.5 MB`) or `2048×2048` (~49 m/sample) lossless 16-bit RG-encoded PNG (`elev = ((R<<8)\|G)/16 - 200`) | O(1) bilinear `HeightmapDEM.sample(x, z)`; `1.0` inside inner `88 km`, cubic-smoothstep blend to procedural world across `44–50 km` (zero seam). |
| **2. Real Airports** | `airports[]` inside `<region>_meta.json` | Real ICAO runway coordinates, true heading, length, elevation | 3–8 regional & international fields per `100 km` grid | Installed via `setCustomAirports()` in `terrain.js` (flattens runway pads, renders 3D runways/lights/hangars, feeds ILS & Kneeboard). |
| **3. Cities, Highways & Landmarks** | `<region>_osm.json` | OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) + curated landmark coordinates | All major cities/towns in the `100 km` box (capped per city by volume, always keeping churches/cathedrals, ~6k–9k buildings total), major highways (`motorway/trunk/primary`), and iconic 3D landmarks (`church`, `bridge`, `dam`, `tower`, `castle`, `lighthouse`) | **Distance-streamed per building** (`BuildingSystem.rebuild` culls to `buildingRadius + alt*5`), OSM buildings cleared off runway strips, dual-carriageway highways capped at `10.5 m` & clipped around airport pads, iconic landmarks rendered permanently with blinking beacons (`LandmarkSystem`). |
| **4. Inland Water Bodies** | `<region>_water.json` | OpenStreetMap (`waterway=river\|canal`, `natural=water`, `waterway=riverbank`) | Major rivers (resampled `25–35 m`) + top ~350 lakes/reservoirs (`> 2,500 m²`) | `InlandWaterSystem` drapes river ribbons (`+0.35 m` above DEM, yielding to sea level `<= 0.4 m`) and triangulates concave lakes (`ShapeUtils.triangulateShape`) with reflective animated water shader + `polygonOffset`. |

## Key Quality & Performance Rules for Future Grids
1. **Horizon Depth Precision**: Always keep `camera.near >= 0.22` (cockpit) / `1.5` (external views) and fade distant water wave normals/specular (`3.5–24 km`) so distant coastlines never Z-fight or shimmer.
2. **Runway Grounding**: Every home airfield must be registered in `setCustomAirports()` with its true magnetic/geographic heading (e.g., `LJAJ` = `090°` East–West) so `terrainHeight()` flattens the runway pad and clears OSM buildings/roads off the strip.
3. **Destructible Real Cities**: Baked OSM buildings flow through the exact same `BuildingSystem` pipeline as procedural cities — wall-aligned reflective glass facades, 4-corner slope grounding, 3D side-wall projectile impacts (`hitBuilding`), animated burn & collapse, multi-piece jagged ruins, and 75-second smoldering fires.
4. **Landmarks Always Visible**: Hilltop churches, major bridges, dams, broadcast towers, castles, and lighthouses are rendered by `LandmarkSystem` with `-4 m` deep foundation skirts so they never float on steep peaks and remain recognizable VFR navigation references from 15+ km away.
5. **Reproducible Bake Scripts**: Keep one Python bake script per layer under `scripts/` (`bake_<region>.py`, `bake_<region>_osm.py`, `bake_<region>_water.py`) so any new `100 × 100 km` theater can be generated by changing `(LAT0, LON0)`, `CITIES`, `airports`, and `RAW_LANDMARKS`.
